Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our commercial kitchen manufacturing, lead times, and support.

What is the typical delivery turnaround time for equipment?

Standard items (single/two burner ranges, standard prep tables, and sinks) are delivered within 3 to 5 working days. Complete custom turnkey restaurant setups with exhaust ducting and custom configurations typically take 10 to 15 working days.

Can you fabricate equipment to custom millimeter dimensions?

Yes, 100%! As a direct manufacturer with in-house CNC shearing and bending machines, we customize width, height, gauge thickness, sink bowl sizes, and burner arrangements to fit your exact kitchen blueprint.

What grade of stainless steel do you use?

We fabricate all commercial food-contact equipment strictly with certified Food-Grade SS 304 (non-magnetic, rust-proof, corrosion-resistant) using heavy 16-gauge and 18-gauge sheets. SS 316 is available upon special industrial request.

Can I visit your factory in Bangalore before purchasing?

Absolutely! We encourage all clients and chefs to visit our factory in Lakshmidevinagar, Bengaluru. You can inspect sheet metal quality, watch live TIG welding, and test burner flame efficiency in person.

Do you provide after-sales warranty and AMC support?

Yes. All manufactured products come with a comprehensive 1-Year Comprehensive Factory Warranty and lifetime engineering support. We also provide Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) with guaranteed 4-hour breakdown turnaround in Bengaluru.
