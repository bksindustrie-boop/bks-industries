About B.K.S. Industries

Welcome to B.K.S. Industries

It is our pleasure to introduce B.K.S. Industries as a leading commercial kitchen equipment manufacturer for diverse foodservice applications — Restaurants, Cafes, Hotels, Cloud Kitchens, Canteens, and Mobile Food Trucks.

Operating from Site No. 22, 1st Cross, Kempamma Layout, Lakshmidevinagar, Bengaluru, we have executed numerous prestigious kitchen projects across Karnataka and South India with heavy-duty SS 304 craftsmanship.

  • Manufactured with heavy food-grade SS 304 with superior corrosion resistance
  • Innovative solutions combining operational efficiency and energy-saving burners
  • Complete commitment in Design, SS Fabrication, Ducting Installation & Support
  • Specialized in custom Mobile Food Trucks & Stainless Steel Conveyor Systems
