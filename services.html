What We Deliver

6 Core Commercial Kitchen Services

Comprehensive solutions handling everything from floor blueprints to hot-line chef commissioning.

1. 2D / 3D CAD Kitchen Architecture

Optimal culinary workflow zoning (Receiving, Cold Prep, Hot Cooking Line, Dishwashing, Storage) to prevent cross-contamination, comply with FSSAI regulations, and maximize kitchen efficiency.

  • Ergonomic Chef Movement Flow
  • Plumbing, Drainage & Electrical Markings

2. Custom SS 304 Equipment Fabrication

Direct factory manufacturing of stockpot ranges, Chinese wok burners, south Indian dosa bhattis, prep tables, wash sinks, bain maries, upright chillers, and custom storage trolleys.

  • 100% Non-Magnetic Food-Grade SS 304
  • Seamless TIG Welding & Mirror Buffing

3. Commercial Exhaust & Fresh Air Ducting

Industrial stainless steel exhaust canopies with removable grease baffle filters, GI ductwork, high-static centrifugal SISW/DIDW blowers, and scrubber systems for smoke-free kitchens.

  • 99% Smoke & Heat Extraction CFM
  • Fresh Air Make-Up Ventilation

4. Commercial LPG / PNG Gas Manifold

Heavy-duty C-Class Seamless MS gas pipelines, dual-stage pressure regulators, cylinder manifold banks, emergency shut-off valves, and automatic gas leak detector sensors.

  • Hydro-Tested Pressure Lines (Up to 10 Bar)
  • PESO & Fire Safety Norms Compliant

5. On-Site Assembly & Commissioning

Safe transport, hoisting, laser alignment, water/gas connection, pilot burner flame testing, and comprehensive chef training before grand opening day.

  • Dedicated On-Site Project Engineer
  • Project Handover On Promised Timeline

6. Annual Maintenance (AMC) & Repairs

Prevent unexpected kitchen downtime with preventive quarterly burner de-greasing, gas pressure calibration, blower belt checks, and guaranteed 4-hour breakdown turnaround in Bengaluru.

  • 100% Genuine Factory Spares in Stock
  • 24/7 Emergency Support Hotline
