Our Heritage & Mission

From Precision Metal Craft to Pan-India Commercial Kitchen Leader

Established in 2010 in Bengaluru, Karnataka, B.K.S. Industries was founded on a simple yet uncompromising principle: build commercial kitchen equipment that withstands the extreme heat, grease, and non-stop operational rigor of high-volume Indian foodservice kitchens.

Over 15+ years, we have expanded from a boutique custom sheet metal fabricator to a full-scale industrial manufacturer powering luxury hotels, multi-chain restaurants, high-throughput cloud kitchens, hospital dietary centers, bakeries, and industrial staff canteens.

  • Certified Heavy-Duty Commercial Grade: 100% rust-proof, non-magnetic austenitic commercial grade equipment with heavy 16G & 18G frames.
  • In-House CNC Fabrication: State-of-the-art hydraulic shearing, CNC bending, TIG welding, and mirror buffing machines.
  • Direct Factory Pricing: Zero middlemen, dealer markups, or broker commissions — buy directly from the manufacturer.
  • Lifetime Engineering AMC Support: Rapid on-site maintenance, genuine replacement spares, and burner recalibration.

The BKS Engineering Standard

Why 500+ restaurateurs and institutional chefs trust BKS equipment over standard market alternatives.

16G Heavy Gauge Heavy-Duty Commercial Grade

We never compromise with cheap 202 grade metal. Every worktop, sink bowl, and cooking chassis is fabricated with certified 16-gauge heavy-duty commercial grade for maximum structural rigidity.

High Thermal Efficiency

Equipped with industrial high-pressure T-35 / M-22 / G-10 heavy cast iron burners engineered to deliver concentrated blue flames, saving up to 18% in commercial LPG gas consumption.

Custom Millimeter Fit

Every kitchen layout has unique dimensions. We conduct laser site measurements and fabricate tables, hoods, and counters to millimeter precision to eliminate dead corner space.

End-to-End Installation

From factory testing to on-site crane hoisting, exhaust duct fitting, manifold connection, and safety handover — our technicians take care of the entire commissioning.
