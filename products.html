Frequently Asked Questions About Our Equipment

1. What commercial grade equipment grade is used in BKS commercial kitchen equipment?

We exclusively manufacture using certified food-grade Heavy-Duty Commercial Grade (16-gauge and 18-gauge) commercial grade equipment for all top surfaces, burner bodies, sinks, work tables, and storage enclosures. Heavy-Duty Commercial Grade provides superior corrosion resistance against acidic foods, high cooking heat, moisture, and cleaning chemicals, ensuring a 15+ year operational lifespan.

2. Can equipment sizes and burner configurations be customized?

Yes, 100%! As a direct manufacturer with our own laser cutting, CNC bending, and argon welding facility in Bengaluru, we customize every single unit according to your kitchen's dimensions, gas pipeline layout (LPG/PNG), and operational workflow requirements.

3. What is the delivery timeline and warranty provided?

Standard catalog products are dispatched within 3 to 7 business days. Custom fabricated equipment orders typically take 10 to 18 days. All equipment is backed by an official 1-Year Comprehensive Factory Warranty and lifetime spare parts & AMC support.

4. Do you also handle commercial exhaust hood ducting and ventilation blowers?

Yes! We manufacture custom Heavy-Duty Commercial Grade baffle filter exhaust canopies, GI/SS heavy ducting networks, centrifugal SISW/DIDW exhaust blowers, and air-washers. Our project engineers calculate CFM air volume requirements and perform on-site commissioning.
